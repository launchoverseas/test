Commercial Engagement Models

We provide three flexible commercial structures aligned with enterprise capitalization, inventory ownership preferences, and operational control requirements.

Model 01 • End-to-End Governance

Full-Scope Turnkey Incubation

Target Profile: High-growth consumer enterprises seeking complete overseas delegation and brand incubation.

Operational Scope
Full top-level brand localization, localized D2C storefronts, Tier-1 Amazon management, offline retail door pitching, influencer campaigns, and complete FDA/CPNP compliance.
Commercial Structure
Base management fee + tiered net revenue share aligned with agreed commercial milestones and verified cross-border sell-through targets.
Inventory Title Governance
Brand retains commercial title until end-consumer delivery or physical retail clearance, maximizing margin preservation and brand equity control.
Marketplace Integration
Synchronized inventory allocation across Amazon North America/Europe, localized Shopify storefronts, and regional department stores.
Model 02 • Rapid Retail Expansion

Regional Distribution Agency

Target Profile: Established brands with proven production scale seeking rapid physical retail doors and regional distributor networks.

Operational Scope
Exclusive territory sales, mass supermarket and specialty cosmetics chain pitching, localized inventory staging, and commercial distributor account management.
Commercial Structure
Wholesale purchase orders (PO) or agreed distributor wholesale margin on scheduled shipment cycles through Hong Kong free-port facilities.
Inventory Title Governance
Distributor takes commercial title upon port handover (FOB HK) or verified destination warehouse inspection, reducing inventory holding risk.
Channel Coverage
Direct placement across Tier-1 chain retailers, specialty beauty stores, regional duty-free hubs, and regional distributor partnerships.
Model 03 • Asset-Light Expansion

Strategic IP Licensing

Target Profile: High-equity consumer brands seeking localized overseas manufacturing, co-branding partnerships, and risk-free regional expansion.

Operational Scope
International common-law trademark licensing, localized production oversight, quality standard audits, packaging compliance, and brand guideline enforcement.
Commercial Structure
Upfront brand licensing royalty + percentage royalty on gross wholesale sales with minimum annual guaranteed remittance.
Inventory Title Governance
Authorized licensee manufactures, finances, and owns 100% of finished goods inventory, creating an asset-light revenue stream for the brand owner.
Legal Governance
Structured under Hong Kong common-law contracts with international intellectual property registration and cross-border patent defense.
