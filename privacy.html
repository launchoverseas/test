Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Effective Date: August 23, 2026. This policy outlines how Launch Overseas Limited manages and protects commercial inquiry data under Hong Kong PDPO, EU GDPR, and international commercial standards.

01. Scope & Core Principles

Institutional B2B Data Handling

Launch Overseas Limited operates strictly as an enterprise consultancy. We do not engage in consumer behavioral tracking or list sales. All data submitted is treated with strict commercial confidence.

02. Data Collected & Methods

Types of Information Processed

We only process data voluntarily submitted by corporate representatives via direct email or official instant advisory channels (WhatsApp), including contact information, brand background, and target expansion scope.

03. Zero-Tracker Policy & Mutual NDA

Zero-Tracker Architecture & Bilateral NDA

Our public website is built on a clean, zero-database static architecture with zero third-party tracking cookies. We routinely execute bilateral Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) prior to receiving proprietary formulation dossiers.
